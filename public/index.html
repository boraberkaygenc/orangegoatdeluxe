Return, risk and tax assumptions

Expected return per instrument: TRY rate + beta × equity premium + a value tilt (earnings yield against the real rate), minus dividend tax; US assets in USD, then converted at your USD/TRY view. US tax: gain in TRY minus ÜFE-indexed cost (when ÜFE ≥ 10%) × your marginal rate.

Language

Labels, headings and buttons switch; generated analysis text stays in English where no translation exists.

Advisor parameters evidence-based defaults

Momentum 12-1 and 6-1 risk-adjusted, value (earnings yield and book-to-market), quality (ROE and margins), low volatility and the 200-day trend make the score; single-stock expectations are shrunk 50% toward the market. Chandelier exit: highest close since entry − multiple × ATR(22). Caps follow the UCITS 5/10/40 rule (10% per stock, positions above 5% together at most 40%) plus a 25% sector cap.

Universe

Extra symbols: any Yahoo Finance ticker outside the index lists — US tickers as they are (NBIS), BIST shares with .IS (TUPRS.IS). They are fetched, scored and gated like the rest and stay until you remove them here.

Constituent lists refresh on the 1st of each month (S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 from public index lists, BIST all shares and index membership from public sources, TEFAS funds by size). Switching a group off removes its instruments unless you hold them.

Data health

Every price carries its source and fetch time (see the As of column in the Screener and the security page). A source that fails twice in a row raises an Inbox warning; failed symbols are retried 10 minutes later.
